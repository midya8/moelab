THE QUANTIFIERS MATTER

One table is not two tables.

The separate bound is max mass of any challenger on its own best table minus min mass of the fixed reference on another table. That subtraction need not be jointly attainable. Tables contain normalized rows, not independent probability boxes.

Declare your own bounds

All five input fields are editable. Use integer or fraction strings, not decimals. Each nominal row must sum to one. Every lower bound must be at most its nominal probability; every upper bound must be at least it. Capacities and eligibility stay fixed across all tables. Edits invalidate the visible result and disable downloads until replay succeeds.
